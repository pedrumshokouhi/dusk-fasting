Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

Dusk does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your fasts, weight, water, mood, measurements, goals and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in a file shared with the Dusk widgets and Apple Watch app. Nothing is sent to me or to any third party. Because the data is not stored in iCloud by Dusk, deleting the app, or using Delete all data in More, removes it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Dusk reads steps, active and resting energy, sleep, heart rate, workouts, height and weight from Apple Health, and can save the weight you log to Apple Health. This data is shown on your device only and is never used for advertising or shared. You can change access at any time in the Health app.

Apple Watch

Fasts sync between your iPhone and your own Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. The data does not pass through my servers.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Exports

If you export a CSV file, it goes wherever you choose to share it. That is your decision and outside of Dusk.

Children

Dusk is not intended for children under 13 and is not suitable for anyone under 18 to use for fasting without medical guidance.
